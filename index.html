Hello and welcome! I am a political scientist who studies state capacity, local governance, and China’s evolving political economy. My previous research has primarily examined how state power shapes local and regional development, creating winners and losers across space. Currently, I am working on three interconnected research streams: (1) bureaucratic control and campaign-style mobilization, (2) intergovernmental collaborative networks, and (3) state–society relations. The common agenda across these projects is to unpack the complex role of the state in governance, development, and innovation—including how it makes a difference and when it backfires.

I received my Ph.D. in political science from The Ohio State University in 2021 and subsequently worked at Fudan and Duke Kunshan University. I am currently teaching at NYU Shanghai.
